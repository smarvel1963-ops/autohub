Auto Hub privacy policy

Auto Hub is an app by Marvel Corp. Last updated October 22, 2026.

The short version

What you type into Auto Hub stays on your phone. We have no Auto Hub account system and no database of your cars. There are no ads, no ad trackers and no analytics in the app. The website version, opened in a web browser, adds one to an anonymous visit count when a page opens - only which page and where the visitor came from, with no cookies, no account and nothing you type. The installed app never does this. A few features send data to other services, and each one is listed below.

What stays on your phone

Your cars and their VINs, mileage, service log, fill-ups, papers (insurance, registration, warranty and so on), extras, notes, bills, to-dos, lists and settings are saved in your browser's storage on your phone. Car photos are kept on your phone only; they are not part of the backup (only a small thumbnail of each car's cover photo is). Reading a VIN barcode with the camera happens on the phone; that picture is not sent anywhere. We cannot see any of it.

You can erase it any time in ⚙ Settings → Your data → Erase everything. Uninstalling the app or clearing its site data also removes everything.

Google user data

Auto Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the features you turned on, in the app, on your phone. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and no person at Marvel Corp reads it. To disconnect, use ⚙ Settings in Auto Hub or remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The backup copy can be deleted from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

Not a mechanic

Service intervals are typical ones, and AI Mechanic answers are possible causes with a rough cost, not a diagnosis. For brakes, steering, smoke, overheating or a flashing check-engine light, pull over safely and get help.

Children

Auto Hub is for adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Selling data

We do not sell or rent personal information. We hold almost none of it.

Changes

If what Auto Hub sends anywhere changes, this page changes first, and the date at the top changes with it.
