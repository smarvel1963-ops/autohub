Using Auto Hub

Auto Hub is a personal car organizer: garage, service log, fuel, recalls, papers and reminders. The free version needs no account. Auto Hub is made for adults. By using it you agree to these terms and to the privacy policy.

Auto Hub Pro

Auto Hub is free for 1 car, with service, fuel, recalls, papers, extras, journal and the build sheet. Auto Hub Pro is a paid subscription of $2.99 a month, with a 30-day free trial. It keeps every car you own and adds backup to your own Google Drive. Pro is sold and billed through Whop. Whop's own terms apply to the purchase, renewal and cancellation. Purchases are made on Whop's website, not through Google Play. The email you bought with unlocks it, on up to 3 phones at a time. A Day Hub Pro purchase also unlocks Auto Hub. The AI helper and Hub AI Credits (one-time packs of 100 questions for $2.99 or 500 for $9.99, usable in any Hub app) are sold the same way, and Settings shows what your plan includes. Free phones that already hold more than 1 car keep them all. If it ends, the paid features stop, and your data and the free features stay.

Your data

Your car information lives on your phone, and in your own Google Drive if you turn on backup (car photos stay on your phone and are not part of the backup). You are responsible for keeping a backup. Phones get lost and browsers get cleared, and we cannot recover data we never had.

Service reminders, recalls and AI Mechanic answers

Service intervals are typical ones and reminders are a guide. Follow your car maker's schedule and your owner's manual, and see a mechanic for anything that matters. AI Mechanic answers are possible causes with a rough cost, not a diagnosis. Recall results come from NHTSA data and may be incomplete, so also check with your dealer. For brakes, steering, smoke, overheating or a flashing check-engine light, pull over safely and get help. Phones can silence notifications and connections drop, so don't rely on Auto Hub alone for a due date. AI helper answers can be wrong, so check anything important.
